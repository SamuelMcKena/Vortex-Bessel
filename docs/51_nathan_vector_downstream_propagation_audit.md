## Field-Type Findings

The current downstream path does **not** reduce the handoff field to scalar intensity before propagation. It keeps `Ex` and `Ey` through the axicon and scalar focal transform, and `propagate_vector_asm` generates/enforces `Ez` through spectral transversality.

The important limitation is narrower:

- the axicon is vector-aware in local radial/azimuthal basis and includes p/s Fresnel amplitudes;
- the z-stack propagation is vector ASM with `k dot E = 0` projection;
- the objective/focal step is not a vectorial Debye/Richards-Wolf model. It is `focus_to_focal_plane` applied independently to `Ex` and `Ey`, then projected by vector ASM at z=0.

That means a poor Nathan-like output cannot be explained as a simple early scalar-intensity collapse. The remaining suspects are scalar focus adequacy, sampling/remapping, chosen micro-scale geometry, and the physical validity of the mechanism in this architecture.
