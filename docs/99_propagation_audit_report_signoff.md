## Canonical evidence convention

For report-facing and long-form research-note evidence, the default paired representation is now:

1. common-scale 2-D transverse intensity sweep at the declared reference plane;
2. fixed-physical-plane x-z and y-z propagation maps where longitudinal propagation is relevant;
3. laboratory-frame `I(x)` and `I(y)` cross-sections to retain steering/decentre information;
4. morphology-axis `I(Delta x)` and `I(Delta y)` cross-sections to compare structural changes independently of steering;
5. common nominal normalisation for primary intensity comparisons;
6. separately labelled own-peak normalisation only when a shape-only supplementary view is useful;
7. axial peak and line-integrated intensity traces where relevant;
8. CSV plus compressed NPZ raw evidence so quantitative claims are not read from PNG pixels.

For even-N grids, physical `x=0` / `y=0` is not assumed to coincide with a native pixel. Profile sampling is performed from the complex angular spectrum at the requested physical coordinate.

## Longitudinal plotting contract

A report-facing x-z/y-z map must represent a **fixed physical plane**. Per-z centroid/core/topology tracking may be used as a separate extracted observable or to select a reference-plane slice, but it must not recenter individual rows of the displayed longitudinal field.

This explicitly supersedes the tracker-warped axicon-v2 presentation that could create artificial step discontinuities by switching between neighbouring Bessel/vortex features.

## Propagation policy

There is no repository-wide rule that one spectral-support strategy is always superior. The accepted rule is:

1. fixed physical plotting coordinates;
2. choose the sampled propagation representation that is independently validated for the relevant field, grid and z range;
3. retain a numerical reference/audit trail.
