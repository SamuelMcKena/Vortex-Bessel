Illustrative Gaussian versus finite-energy Bessel fields with matched transverse FWHM. Use an explicitly labelled short axial zoom for the Gaussian and a longer Bessel-range view; independently normalised to each peak.

### 2. `fig2_2_bessel_vortex_families`
Analytical scalar fields with topological orders 0, 1, 3 and 20: radial intensity, transverse intensity, and wrapped phase. These are ideal-field references, not camera recordings.

### 3. `fig3_1_asm_validation_screening`
Numerical angular-spectrum versus analytical reference test at declared grid/window; include error curves and convergence. Do not describe as validated until mismatch and Nyquist guard failures are corrected.

### 4. `fig3_2_ideal_beam_family`
Ideal scalar Bessel-0 and vortex charges 1 and 3, with transverse maps, longitudinal sections, and radial functions compared against Bessel analytical profiles.

### 5. `fig3_3_parameter_analytic_sweeps`

### 6. `fig3_4_pupil_geometry`
Geometric effect of finite Gaussian-illumination aperture on the available conical-wave overlap and expected Bessel zone. Does not reproduce diffraction-driven axial ripple.

### 7. `fig3_5_self_healing`

### 8. `fig3_6_vector_jones_fields`

### 9. `fig4_0_axicon_angle_diagnostic`

### 10. `fig4_1_component_phase_and_camera`

### 11. `fig4_2_field_through_4f_relay`

### 12. `fig4_3_ideal_vs_camera_integrated`

### 13. `fig4_4_pointing_Snell_sweep`

### 14. `fig4_5_gaussian_decentre`

### 15. `fig4_6_slm2_vortex_registration`

### 17. `fig4_8_lens_decentre_phase`

### 18. `fig4_9_ring_fit_vs_centroid`

### 19. `fig4_10_axicon_oblique_rays`

### 20. `fig4_11_rounded_tip_sag`
